A new pickup request comes in at 2 p.m., and the nearest vehicle is halfway through its route. It has already dropped off several orders and collected a couple of returns, so the dispatcher has to work out whether the extra load will fit. The planned weight on the route doesn't answer that, because it describes the vehicle at departure.
