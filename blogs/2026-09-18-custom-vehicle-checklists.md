A driver taking out a refrigerated truck has more to inspect than a colleague in a standard van: the cooling unit has to hold its set temperature, and someone should record that before the first drop. Until now, Track-POD applied one checklist to the whole fleet, so a check like that ended up either on every driver's list or on nobody's. The latest update adds custom checklist templates for Ultimate plan users.
