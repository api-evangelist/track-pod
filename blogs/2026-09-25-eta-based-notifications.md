A route that starts on Monday morning and ends on Wednesday afternoon puts dispatchers in an awkward spot with customer notifications. If the message goes out when the route starts, the customer on the last stop hears about their delivery two days early. A fixed time of day doesn't help much either, since stops on the same route can be a day or more apart.
