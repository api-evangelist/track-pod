Many shipments don't go straight from the sender to the recipient. A driver collects the goods and brings them to a hub, where they're sorted, stored or consolidated, and later another route takes them on to the customer. In Track-POD, this is a Pickup-Hub-Delivery order: two orders, one for each leg, that often run on different routes and different days.
